# Phantom Blade Zero Surpasses 4 Million Copies in 72 Hours: Steam Peak Hits 833,000

<p><em>November 2, 2026 · Phantom Blade Zero · 8 min read</em></p>

Over its very first weekend following the October 29 global release, S-GAME's Unreal Engine 5 dark wuxia action RPG **Phantom Blade Zero** shattered commercial records. S-GAME officially announced that the game has **surpassed 4 million units sold across all platforms** (PS5, PS5 Pro, and PC) within just **72 hours** of launch. Fueled by an enthusiastic weekend surge, its Steam peak concurrent player count soared to **833,655**, securing an unshakeable spot atop the Steam Global Top Sellers chart.

## 72 Hours, 4 Million Copies: Over 1 Billion RMB in Revenue

Priced at $59.99 for Standard and $69.99 for Deluxe editions globally (268 RMB / 328 RMB in China), *Phantom Blade Zero* generated more than **1 billion RMB** ($140M+ USD) in gross revenue within its first three days.

This performance allowed developer S-GAME to completely recoup its multi-year production and marketing budget of approximately 300 to 400 million RMB in a mere 72 hours. Industry market analysts highlight that international sales across North America, Europe, Japan, and Korea accounted for nearly **45%** of all sales, affirming the massive global appeal of premium Chinese action titles.

## Steam Weekend Surge: 833,000 Peak Sets Action RPG Record

Following a steady rise during its Thursday debut, the weekend saw a massive influx of international players.

At 833,655 concurrent players, *Phantom Blade Zero* now ranks among the highest-peaking single-player action RPGs in Steam history.

## Reviews Rebound to 84%: Winning the Optimization Race

During the early hours of launch, shader compilation stuttering and occasional driver crashes caused Steam user reviews to hover between 76% and 79%.

However, following S-GAME's quick graphics recommendations, the deployment of the 5GB day-one patch, and players unlocking the masterfully designed mid-to-late game boss battles, sentiment rebounded strongly to **84% Very Positive** across more than **42,000 user reviews**. Critics and action gaming veterans praised the game's lightning-fast parry counters, visceral dismemberment feedback, and deep roster of over 50 bosses.

## Dark Kungfupunk: Violent Lethal Arts Meet Feminine Grace

*Phantom Blade Zero* establishes a distinctive "Kungfupunk" visual signature. The Unreal Engine 5 production combines relentless sword clashes, bone-shattering impacts, and atmospheric blood mist with a dark oriental aesthetic.

Contrasting against the violent metal-on-metal combat is the game's captivating feminine elegance. The enigmatic white-haired female swordswoman, central to both the main narrative and multiple hidden sidequests, displays a calm, detached grace amid violent storms. This sharp aesthetic dichotomy has sparked widespread community praise and fan creations across Twitter, YouTube, and Bilibili.

## Producer Liang Qiwei's Open Letter: Patch 1.02 & 1.03 Timeline

On the fourth day post-launch, S-GAME founder and director **Liang Qiwei** published a heartfelt open letter thanking players worldwide. He shared that the studio operated around the clock throughout the first 72 hours:

> "Thank you to every player who supported us in transforming a 20-year-old dream into reality. The cold steel belongs to you. We are fully aware of shortcomings in performance stability and controller customization. Our entire team is working relentlessly without rest to deliver fixes."

S-GAME confirmed the upcoming patch roadmap:
- **Patch 1.02 (Deploying within 24–48 hours):** Background shader pre-caching optimization, memory leak fixes in rain environments, and frame rate stabilization on PS5 Performance Mode.
- **Patch 1.03 (Targeted for this week):** Full custom keyboard/mouse remapping, 21:9 ultrawide HUD scaling improvements, and customizable gamepad deadzone sliders.

## Industry Significance: Another Milestone for Chinese Single-Player Gaming

From the explosive arrival of *Black Myth: Wukong*, to *WUCHANG: Fallen Feathers* reaching 5 million players, to *Phantom Blade Zero* achieving 4 million sales and 833K concurrent players in just three days, China's premium single-player AAA development has proven its enduring global viability and enormous commercial reach.
