## Release and unlock time

- **October 29, 2026 at 10:00 AM Beijing time (02:00 UTC)** on PS5, PS5 Pro and PC via Steam, Epic Games Store, WeGame and TapTap PC.
- **China pricing:** Standard Edition ¥268, Deluxe Edition ¥328.
- **No Xbox version at launch.** The PS5 physical release is due in **2027**, in standard, deluxe and collector's steelbook editions.
- **Preload opened on October 27 at 10:00** Beijing time, simultaneously on PS5 and PC.

## Reviews: Metacritic 88, a new high for Chinese single-player

The embargo lifted on **October 26**, and the verdict is even stronger than the final previews suggested.

- **Metacritic:** **88** on PC (44 critics), **86** on PS5 (34 critics) after opening at 87.
- **OpenCritic:** opened at **91** and has settled around **89**, with a near-100% recommendation rate.
- **Context:** above *Black Myth: Wukong* (81), *Nioh 3* (86) and *Onimusha: Way of the Sword* (85). Outlets describe it as the highest-rated PS5 console exclusive of 2026.

Reports count **ten perfect scores** in total; former Sony Worldwide Studios head **Shuhei Yoshida** shared TheGamer's 5/5 review.

**What critics agree on:**

- **The combat is the headline.** Parries, "killing intent" and Ghost Step counters drew near-universal praise, and 55+ weapons plus distinctive bosses keep every fight from having a single answer.
- **Art direction and level design beat expectations.** The "kungfupunk" identity and the density of the semi-open maps are judged well above earlier Chinese AAA releases.
- Several outlets call it "one of the best action games ever made" and a 2026 Game of the Year contender.

**Where they split:**

- **Thin story.** A predictable revenge plot and flat characters are the most common complaints.
- **Linear levels and invisible walls** leave less freedom than the marketing implied.
- **Technical issues.** Frame drops during heavy rain and some boss fights on PS5, plus stutter on PC with ray tracing or path tracing enabled.
- **Eurogamer's 3/5** is the lone low score, citing balance and overall polish outside combat.

## The final trailer: old moves return

On **October 21–22**, S-GAME released a **3 minute 10 second** final trailer. Beyond the story beats and combat footage, the highlights were:

- **3D remakes of legacy moves:** Phantom Kill (绝影「绝杀」) and the Fire Dragon Tornado (炽刃「火龙卷」).
- **More screen time for Zuo Shang**, whose hair turns from black to white; the trailer opens with him conspiring with "Lord Huang."
- **Returning characters** Chang Hong and Lei Dang, plus new weapons: a green-flame meteor hammer, a bronze sword, a drunken sword and a long-handled modao.
- **Boss fights:** Mu Xiaokui's new fist form (with Yu Yao and Tie Gui), Chang Hong's lancer phase, a duel between two ghost messengers and a Lei Dang finale.
- **Donnie Yen** plays Moyuan, the father of protagonist Soul, and serves as creative consultant; his stunt team handled motion capture.

## Preload and PC requirements: 145GB, but reserve 300GB

- The PC build is about **145GB**, but preloading requires **at least 300GB** free — the compressed preload and the unpacked game briefly coexist, and usage drops back to roughly 145GB after unlock.
- An **SSD** is required, and the official guidance is to avoid the C: drive.
- **PS5 download size: 134.857GB.** Digital only; the disc version arrives in 2027.
- Launch support includes ray-traced reflections, shadows and caustics, plus **DLSS 4.5** upscaling and multi-frame generation. NVIDIA recommends driver **621.01**.

## Golden Joystick Game of the Year nomination

On **October 26**, the 2026 Golden Joystick Awards (44th edition) announced 12 nominees for **Ultimate Game of the Year**, and *Phantom Blade Zero* is the **only Chinese single-player AAA title** on the list. The field also includes *007 First Light*, *Crimson Desert*, *Forza Horizon 6*, *Resident Evil Requiem* and *The Blood of Dawnwalker*. *GTA 6* is absent, and no Sony first-party title made the cut.

- **Voting closes:** October 30 at 23:59 GMT (**October 31, 07:59 Beijing time**) at goldenjoysticks.com.
- **Ceremony:** **November 11** at the Troxy in London, streamed worldwide.
- The game launches on October 29, after the usual eligibility cutoff; it was admitted because the jury had already played review builds, which leaves players only about two days to vote.

::: info Why it was allowed in
Golden Joystick nominees are normally expected to be out before voting closes. The jury had already played review builds in full, so an exception was made — which is why the public vote window is only two days long.
:::

## What to watch on launch day

- **The day-one patch and real PC performance** — whether the review-build stutter is gone.
- **PS5 Pro performance**, and whether the 12-month console exclusivity is ever officially confirmed.
- **Steam concurrents.** Pre-orders topped the global bestseller chart within about an hour; day-one numbers will be another marker for Chinese single-player games.
- **Post-launch support.** There is still no DLC roadmap.
