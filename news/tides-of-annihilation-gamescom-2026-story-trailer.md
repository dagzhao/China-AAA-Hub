## The "Beneath the Veil" story trailer

At **Gamescom Opening Night Live on August 25, 2026**, Eclipse Glow premiered **"Beneath the Veil,"** the game's first story trailer.

- It introduces **Guinevere**, a white-haired figure who teaches Gwendolyn to command the **Fog**.
- **Lancelot** makes his debut among the spectral Knights of the Round Table.
- The trailer shows aerial combat and the fractured modern London/Avalon setting.
- Wishlisting opened on **PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Steam and Epic Games Store** the same week.

## The 45-minute demo

From **August 26–30**, Eclipse Glow ran the first European public hands-on at **Hall 6, Booth B-050**. The demo included:

- A story mission through ruined London.
- Multi-phase boss fights against **Mordred** (labelled "medium" difficulty), **Anubis** in the Great Russell Museum, and **Tyronoe**.
- The **Dual Frontline Battle System**: Gwendolyn fights while commanding spectral knights, with two loadouts and main/support slots that can be swapped mid-combo.
- **Niniane**, a companion who can transform into a sword.

Press impressions were strong. DualShockers called it "an action-adventure for the ages"; Wccftech described a fast, DMC-like game rather than a Soulslike. The main open questions were performance and how the knight system scales over a full campaign.

The game won **"Most Epic"** at the Gamescom Awards 2026.

## A new voice for Gwendolyn

**Angela Sant'Albano** (*Resident Evil Requiem*'s Grace Ashcroft) is now the voice of **Gwendolyn**. She replaces **Jennifer English** (*Clair Obscur: Expedition 33*), who stepped down earlier in 2026 for personal health reasons but remains involved as an advisor.

## Release date: still TBA

Director **Ary Chen** declined to give a release date at Gamescom, and also declined to confirm playtime or boss-count figures. He said development is ongoing and the team is gathering feedback.

- The **February 2025 announcement** gave a tentative **2026** window.
- Some storefronts show **October 29, 2026** — a **placeholder**, not an official date.
- Media speculation now mostly points to **2027**; IGN and Push Square both say "not before 2027."
- There is **no official date**, and no official delay announcement — the game has simply never had one.
- The 45-minute demo is event-only; the next stop is **Beijing Fusion on October 24–25, 2026 (Booth A06)**.

## Platforms and features

- **PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC** (Steam, Epic Games Store).
- **Xbox Play Anywhere** confirmed.
- **No Switch 2 version** currently planned, according to the director.
- **Unreal Engine 5**, with PC path tracing and **DLSS 4** support.
- **11 languages**, including Brazilian Portuguese; Chinese voice-over and text are available.
- Self-published by Eclipse Glow Games, a Chengdu studio of roughly 115–150 people; Tencent is the majority investor.

## What to watch next

- Whether the 2026 window survives into next year.
- More detail on the **30+ bosses** and **30+ hour campaign** figures reported by Chinese media.
