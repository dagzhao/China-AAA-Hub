# Where Winds Meet 2.3 Qingzhou: New Region, Inkbound Order & DLSS 5

<p><em>Published October 21, 2026 · Where Winds Meet · 7 min read</em></p>

*Where Winds Meet* opened its first "thematic region" on **October 15, 2026** with **Version 2.3, "Friends From Afar."** Qingzhou is smaller than a full major region but denser in systems: a new school, a new weapon, a card-debate PvP mode and three world bosses. It arrives three weeks after the Hidden Mountain finale and two months after the game passed 100 million registered players.

## What 2.3 adds

### Qingzhou

The game's fifth major area, covering **Mount Tai**, **Wellspring Town**, **Crownleek Terrace** and the **Yellow River estuary**. The zone is built around the idea of a scholarly pilgrimage: academies, mountain roads and river trade rather than a single battlefield.

### The Inkbound Order (文津馆)

A new school that introduces a **dual-school / visiting-disciple system** — you can study under the Inkbound Order without abandoning your original sect. It also adds **"A Duel of Wits,"** a card-debate PvP mode.

### Ribbon Drum (舞绫鼓)

A new weapon paired with the **Silkbind: Wings (牵丝·翊)** style. Long ribbons handle crowd control; drum beats buff allies. It is the most support-oriented weapon added so far.

### Three world bosses

- **Tidewatcher (观潮客)**
- **Old Raftsman (筏师的爷爷)**
- **Shuttlecock Champion (祝英晗)**

### Progression and events

- Solo level cap raised from **17 to 18**.
- Season 5: **"Ever Aflame."**
- **Qingzhou opening month + Exam Showdown:** October 15 – November 14.
- **New Armament Drill:** October 15 – 29.
- **Bubble Party / Merchant's Way:** October 22.
- **Penglai Isle + Mount Tai Blizzard campaign:** October 29.

### DLSS 5

NVIDIA announced on **October 13–14** that *Where Winds Meet* will receive full **DLSS 5 (3D-Guided Neural Rendering)** on PC by **late November**. It is one of the first three games in the world to support it. At launch, 2.3 did not include DLSS 5, which led to some "paper promise" complaints.

## The 2.2 finale, in case you missed it

Version 2.2 **"Perilous Eminence"** launched on **September 16** and closed the Hidden Mountain storyline:

- **Skyward City Ruins:** ride wooden kites through collapsing towers and face the previous Grand Master, **Qing**.
- **Final Direction:** a battlefield where gravity keeps reversing, ending in a duel with **Elder Peng**.
- **Qianye** returns as the finale's antagonist.
- **Golden Colossus Taicu:** tied to Qin Shi Huang's twelve golden statues; destroy the field structures to lift its seal, then climb its body to break the core.
- **Goshawk:** a hammer-wielding boss with molten-iron "flower" attacks.
- **Transdust Ruins (September 23):** a four-phase extraction mode built with an official **Escape from Tarkov** collaboration. Requires level 20+; supports solo, three-player or AI teams; disguises, pickpocketing and acupoint strikes are usable.
- **Drunken Fist** arrived on September 3, alongside a **Yuen Woo-ping** choreography collaboration.
- The **Chilled Current** battle pass ended on October 15.

## The China server is ahead

The Chinese client runs a faster schedule and is roughly **six months ahead** of the international build:

- **2.8.5 "平澜起风波" (September 24):** completes Hangzhou with the **Pinglan Academy** and **Yingtian Pagoda**, plus the mulberry-sea and Qiantang embankment areas. Main story plus five side stories exceed **500 minutes**; new guardian **Qiantang Tide** and world boss **Angry Tiger**. The client also shed nearly **100 GB on PC** and **20 GB on mobile**.
- **"长乐夜未央" (October 23):** the second thematic region, reached by "golden ship" from Hangzhou. It adds **four subregions, two guardians, three trails and five world bosses**, including the game's **first freely explorable underwater world**, a tide cycle and a black market. The theme song **"宝船"** is by **DOUDOU**.

## The audience

- **100 million cumulative registered players** announced on **August 26, 2026**, up from 80 million+ in June. This is registrations, not monthly active users.
- Mobile revenue of about **$223.7 million** by **June 16, 2026** (AppMagic estimate).
- Steam all-time peak around **250,600** at the November 2025 launch; **October 2026 peak 20,678**, monthly average about **8,647**.
- Steam rating **86–87% positive** across 113,000–116,000 reviews; Metacritic around **71** from critics, **6.9–7.2** from users.
- **Best Mobile Game** at Gamescom 2026.

## Reception of 2.3

The main criticisms are that Qingzhou feels more like a **"themed mini-map"** than a full region, and that performance and shader-compilation stutters remain on some PCs. DLSS 5 was not available at launch. The positive side: the Inkbound Order and Ribbon Drum give the game two genuinely new playstyles, and the 2.2 finale is widely regarded as the strongest story content of the year.

## What to watch next

- **DLSS 5** on PC by late November.
- **Penglai Isle** and the Mount Tai Blizzard campaign on October 29.
- Whether the international build begins to close the **six-month gap** with the China server.
- The next major region after Qingzhou — not yet announced.
