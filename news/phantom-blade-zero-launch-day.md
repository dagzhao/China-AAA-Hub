# Phantom Blade Zero Launch Day: 720K Steam Peak, 83% Positive

<p><em>Published October 30, 2026 · Phantom Blade Zero · 8 min read</em></p>

*Phantom Blade Zero* unlocked on PS5, PS5 Pro and PC at 10:00 AM Beijing time on October 29. A day later, the numbers are in: a Steam concurrent peak of roughly **720,000**, good enough for No. 15 on the all-time chart and the top of the global bestseller list. But technical problems kept Steam reviews in the 70s for much of the day — this was a launch defined by a race between reputation and optimization.

## Day-one concurrents: a ~720K peak, No. 15 all-time

Within **20 minutes** of unlock, concurrent players passed **100,000** and the game topped Steam's global bestseller chart. It kept climbing for about **13 hours** before hitting its day-one peak.

| Time (Beijing) | Steam concurrents |
| :--- | :--- |
| ~10:20 (20 min after unlock) | 100,000+, No. 1 on the global bestseller chart |
| 13:30 | 431,541 |
| Afternoon | 450,000+ |
| 20:00 | 600,000+ |
| 22:00 | 700,000+ |
| ~23:00 | ~720,000 (peak 723,876), still 680,000+ online |

- **No. 15 all-time.** Next up is *Battlefield 6* at 747,440, then *Baldur's Gate 3* at 875,343 (No. 13). Given the curve, the weekend could push it higher.
- **One of 2026's strongest single-player launches.** Outlets note it is far above *Resident Evil Requiem* (~344K) and *Forza Horizon 6* (~303K).
- **Still far from *Black Myth: Wukong*.** Wukong's Steam peak was about 2.4 million — the benchmark for Chinese single-player games.
- The launch fell on a Thursday, so the **weekend peak** is expected to rise further.

## Steam reviews: 83% positive, and the complaints are all about performance

User reviews opened at **76–79%** and climbed to **83%** by late evening (~23,400 reviews) — a "Mostly Positive" rating.

- **The negatives are strikingly consistent, and unrelated to design:** crashes, slow shader compilation, long load times, unstable frame rates, compatibility issues with Nvidia driver 621.01, and hair/sky rendering glitches on AMD cards.
- **The positives:** combat impact, weapon feel, fluid combos and the "golden-age Hong Kong cinema" staging.
- Critics and players broadly agree this is a game **held back by hardware compatibility**, not by its content — which means the score can recover with patches.

## The 5GB day-one patch: better ray tracing, and a driver rollback

The PC day-one patch is about **5GB** (PS5 receives its own build). It contains no story, gameplay or balance changes; the focus is on **significantly improving ray-tracing and path-tracing performance**, fixing bugs and polishing rasterized visuals.

More unusually, S-GAME reversed its driver advice. Before launch it recommended Nvidia **621.01**; on launch day it said that driver has "unstable performance in certain situations" and advised players to **clean-install 617.42** or wait for the next driver.

::: tip Before you launch the game
Install the day-one patch first. On Nvidia, if you are running driver **621.01**, S-GAME recommends a clean install of **617.42** before playing.
:::

## PS5 and PC performance on day one

Based on first-day reports from reviewers and players:

| Platform | Day-one performance |
| :--- | :--- |
| PS5 · Performance mode | Close to 60 fps in most scenes; drops in rain sequences and some boss fights, with occasional one-second hitches and texture pop-in |
| PS5 · Quality mode | Struggles on frame rate; not the recommended option |
| PS5 Pro | The most stable — performance mode with ray tracing holds close to 60 fps |
| PC · RTX 5060 | Roughly 41–45 fps at native 1080p with ray tracing |
| PC · RTX 5090D V2 | Cannot hold 60 fps at native 4K path tracing; DLSS/FSR is effectively required |

The official spec table already assumes upscaling. Whether the 5GB patch fixes the frame-time issues noted in reviews will need another weekend of testing.

## Critics 88, players 83%: why the gap

Critic scores have held: Metacritic shows **88** on PC (45 critics) and **86** on PS5 (38), with OpenCritic around **90**. IGN gave 9/10, IGN China 10/10, GameSpot 8/10, and Eurogamer's 3/5 remains the lone low mark.

- **What critics praise:** the parry-and-"killing intent" duel system, Ghost Step counters, and the feel of 55-plus weapons.
- **What critics criticize:** a predictable revenge plot, invisible walls and linear level design.
- **What players criticize:** crashes, shaders and driver compatibility. Some also find it too easy — but this was never a Soulslike.
- The conclusion is clear: **the recovery depends on patches and drivers, not on content.**

## What kung fu punk looks like in motion

The part of *Phantom Blade Zero* that needs no patch is how it looks and moves. Action design by **Kenji Tanigaki** (fight choreographer on the live-action *Rurouni Kenshin* films) and Donnie Yen's team gives the steel real weight, with cloth-and-rain physics and one-versus-many camera work that players have called "a playable Hong Kong film."

## Chinese game industry passes the torch

On launch day, **Feng Ji** — producer of *Black Myth: Wukong* — published a long congratulatory post. He recalled knowing Liang Qiwei for years and called their paths "different roads to the same place." He wrote that "single-player games are not a higher form of game," that Chinese single-player creators face "a better era, and a harder one," and closed with **"桃李不言，功不唐捐"** — good work speaks for itself, and effort is never wasted. "I'll hand my time to *Phantom Blade Zero*," he added. Liang replied: "Thank you. Enjoy the game."

- On **October 20**, Feng Ji had posted "nine days left, and every day feels like a year"; on launch eve he commented under Liang's post: "Preloaded seriously, alarm set."
- NetEase's *Guitang* team sent congratulatory art and gave all 24 team members a copy of the game.
- *Delta Force* announced a crossover with *Phantom Blade Zero*, and PlayStation China swapped its banner to the game.
- The credits list Ma Xiaoyi, Feng Ji, Liu Wei, Li Songlun, Shuhei Yoshida, Tim Sweeney, Kim Hyung-tae and others.

## ~850K pre-orders, still no official sales number

- Analyst firm **Alinea Analytics** estimates about **850,000 digital pre-orders** worth roughly **$43 million**: **73.8%** on Steam and **26.2%** on PS5, with about **62.5%** of buyers in Greater China.
- Some outlets report total sales above **1 million** once WeGame is included, but **S-GAME has not announced official figures**.
- The reported budget is RMB **300–400 million**. China pricing is ¥268 standard, ¥328 deluxe, and ¥1,199 for a 20,000-unit collector's edition.
- Golden Joystick "Ultimate Game of the Year" voting closes **October 31 at 07:59 Beijing time**; the ceremony is **November 11**.

## What to watch next

- **The weekend peak** — Thursday was only the opening day.
- **Official sales milestones** — first-week numbers will define the "next Chinese AAA" narrative.
- **Whether reviews climb back to 90%** — it depends on patches and drivers.
- **PS5 Pro performance**, and whether the 12-month console exclusivity is officially confirmed.
- **A DLC roadmap** — still unannounced.
- **The Golden Joystick Awards** on November 11, where it was nominated before release.
