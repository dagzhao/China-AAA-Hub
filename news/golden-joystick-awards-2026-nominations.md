# Golden Joystick Awards 2026: Phantom Blade Zero Nominated for Ultimate GOTY, Zhong Kui for Most Wanted

<p><em>November 10, 2026 · Awards & Industry · 6 min read</em></p>

Chinese single-player blockbusters have secured an extraordinary double triumph on the international stage. In the official nominees roster for the **44th Golden Joystick Awards (2026)**, S-GAME's Unreal Engine 5 kungfupunk action RPG **Phantom Blade Zero** earned a coveted nomination for **Ultimate Game of the Year**, the show's top honor. Simultaneously, Game Science's upcoming dark mythological title **Black Myth: Zhong Kui** received a nomination for **Most Wanted Game**.

## 1. Phantom Blade Zero: The Road to Ultimate GOTY

Following its **October 29, 2026** launch across PlayStation 5, PS5 Pro, and PC, *Phantom Blade Zero* took the gaming world by storm:
- **Commercial & critical breakthrough:** Over **4 million copies sold in 72 hours**, generating upwards of **1 billion RMB** ($140M+ USD) and recouping its entire development and promotional budget within three days.
- **Steam milestone:** An opening-weekend peak concurrent player count of **833,655**, securing a spot among the top single-player action titles in Steam history.
- **Critique & acclaim:** Metacritic scores opened at **88**, with universal praise directed at its hand-crafted motion choreography (supervised by martial arts veteran Donnie Yen), dynamic parrying mechanics, and responsive "Kungfupunk" aesthetics.

The Golden Joystick Awards committee highlighted *Phantom Blade Zero*'s sheer combat dynamism and technical polish, granting it a spot on the 12-title Ultimate GOTY ballot alongside major international blockbusters.

## 2. Black Myth: Zhong Kui Nominated for Most Wanted Game

Following Game Science's historic debut of the **15-minute gameplay demo on August 20, 2026**, anticipation for *Black Myth: Zhong Kui* has reached a fever pitch worldwide:
- **Gameplay reveal impact:** Over **10 million views** across Bilibili, Weibo, and YouTube within 48 hours, highlighting a switch from Wukong's staff to a single-handed parrying sword, talisman enchantments, and ghost summoning.
- **Industry recognition:** The Golden Joystick Awards jury officially shortlisted *Zhong Kui* for **Most Wanted Game**, cementing Game Science's position as one of the most closely watched single-player developers in the world.

## 3. Global Voting & What Comes Next

Public voting for the Golden Joystick Awards has concluded, with the final results and trophy ceremonies broadcast live from London on **November 11, 2026**.

The double nomination marks a historic moment for China's expanding AAA gaming industry, proving that culturally distinctive, high-budget single-player projects can consistently achieve both commercial velocity and critical legitimacy across western and global gaming spheres.
