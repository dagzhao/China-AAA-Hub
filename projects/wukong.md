# Black Myth: Wukong

> 📰 **Latest:** [Wukong DLC Status: Paused, Not Cancelled](/news/black-myth-wukong-dlc-status)

## 1. Myth Reconstructed: The Destined One
"Black Myth: Wukong" is a dark, cinematic reimagining of the classic *Journey to the West*. Players take on the role of the "Destined One," retracing the footsteps of the Great Sage through perilous landscapes to uncover the truth buried within the ruins of legends.

## 2. Combat Philosophy: Powers of the Sage
The gameplay focuses on recreating the supernatural abilities of Chinese mythology rather than traditional parry-heavy mechanics.
* **72 Transformations**: Absorb the souls of defeated elite foes to transform into their forms and utilize their unique skills.
* **Spells & Arts**: Manipulate the battlefield with Taoist arts like "Immobilize" and "Cloud Step".
* **Staff Mastery**: Fluidly switch between Smash, Pillar, and Thrust stances to adapt to any threat.

## 3. Two-Year Anniversary: 30% Off — and a Zhong Kui Gameplay Reveal

**August 20, 2026** marked the **second anniversary** of *Black Myth: Wukong*. All digital editions were **30% off** from **August 12 to August 25** (PS Store through August 26) — the game's first 30% discount since launch. In China, the Steam Standard Edition fell from **¥268** to **¥187.6**, the Deluxe Edition from **¥328** to **¥229.6**; in the US, the Standard Edition went from **$59.99** to **$41.99**.

> 🎉 **What the "Big News" actually was:** not Wukong DLC. At **10:00 AM Beijing Time on August 20**, Game Science released a **15-minute gameplay demo of *Black Myth: Zhong Kui*** across Bilibili, Weibo, YouTube and its official site. Recorded in 21:9 from an early build, it showed a white-robed swordsman, parry-and-deflect combat, fire and lightning sword enchantments, talismans, summonable ghost helpers and short possession mechanics, ending with the first named boss, **Great Clam Lord (大蚌真君)**. No release date was given.

**Reception:** the demo passed **1 million Bilibili views in under an hour**, **3 million in three hours**, and **10 million within two days** (about 829,000 likes). On Weibo the topic exceeded **130 million reads**; on YouTube it drew **1.9 million views in 24 hours**. Geoff Keighley also shared it on X.

**A series rebrand:** the *Wukong* official account was renamed simply **"Black Myth" (黑神话)** the same day — widely read as the first move toward a shared *Black Myth* universe.

Nothing else Wukong-related — no DLC, sequel, complete edition, Switch 2 port, film or patch — was announced that day. Producer **Feng Ji** published a long post on the studio's "ten working principles," while art director **Yang Qi** explained why the team chose a new game over Wukong DLC.

## 4. The DLC Question: Paused, Not Cancelled

Wukong's DLC has been **on hold since August 20, 2025**, when Game Science announced *Zhong Kui* instead. Feng Ji said the team had spent more than half a year on DLC plans before deciding to start something new:

> "Wukong's legend will return in a more complete, more solid form — when it is ready." — Feng Ji (translated from his August 2025 post)

He also quoted Nintendo's Satoru Iwata: **"There is no future on the existing extension line."**

On **August 20, 2026**, Yang Qi expanded on the decision: the internal DLC proposals **"lacked creative passion"** and felt **less compelling than fan creations**; a DLC would also inherit the base game's technical compromises — lighting, invisible walls and combat camera work. The team wants to use *Zhong Kui* to rebuild its fundamentals first.

As of **October 21, 2026**, there is still **no DLC, season pass, roadmap or release window**. Store listings offer only the Deluxe Upgrade and soundtrack — no story content. The widely circulated **"Six Desires" (六欲劫)** DLC rumor has been debunked; no official source exists.

## 5. After the Anniversary: Concerts, Xbox Comparisons and a 30-Million Question

- **Sales, with a caveat** — On **October 4**, CCTV's culture-export report repeated the **"30 million copies"** figure. It originated from a **June 15** think-tank report, not Game Science; in July the studio said the number **"differs from our internal data"** and confirmed only that overseas sales are about half. The last official hard figure remains **10 million copies in three days** (August 2024).
- **Xbox comparison (September 18)** — A technical analysis by El Analista De Bits found that **Xbox Series X performance mode** runs at a higher average resolution than PS5, but with noticeably reduced shadows and global illumination; **Series S** cuts lighting, shadows, draw distance and vegetation. The verdict: "satisfactory except for the XSX performance mode."
- **Patches** — The last confirmed official patch was **1.0.21.23831** in mid-January 2026. In September, SteamDB spotted three **AMD FidelityFX DLLs** added to a depot, sparking ray-reconstruction speculation; no official update followed, and there was **no major September–October patch**.
- **Concerts** — The 2026 global soundtrack tour (12 cities, 21 shows) played two sold-out nights in **Taipei on September 19–20**, with about 19 pieces including *Cloud Palace Quickstep*, *Where the Road Leads* and *Unfinished*, plus a *Zhong Kui* theme encore. **Singapore's Esplanade Theatre** hosts the next stop on **October 24–25**.
- **Merch** — BLACKMYTH's second autumn merchandise wave went on sale **September 30**: lantern phone straps, the Kunqi hoodie, *Do Not Listen* storyboard long-sleeves, mousepads, gourd pendants and a Hangzhou-store-exclusive Xiaohuanglong magnet figure.
- **Still no new edition** — No annual or complete edition, season pass or new discount appeared in September–October; the anniversary 30% sale remains the year's deepest. **Switch 2** remains an unconfirmed rumor, and no film or animation adaptation has been announced.
