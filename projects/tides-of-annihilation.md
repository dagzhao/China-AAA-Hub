# Tides of Annihilation: Release Date, Story & Gameplay

> 📰 **Latest:** [Gamescom 2026 story trailer and hands-on impressions](/news/tides-of-annihilation-gamescom-2026-story-trailer)

**Tides of Annihilation** is a single-player Arthurian action-adventure from Chinese studio **Eclipse Glow Games**. It is set in a modern London shattered by an invasion from the Otherworld, and it is one of the most anticipated Chinese AAA titles still without a release date.

## 2. What is Tides of Annihilation?

*Tides of Annihilation* was announced at Sony's **State of Play on February 13, 2025**. It is a buy-to-play, single-player action game — not a live-service title and not a Soulslike. The studio describes it as a fast, cinematic action-adventure in the vein of *Devil May Cry* and *Bayonetta*, with *God of War* as a secondary influence.

The game is built around two ideas: a **post-apocalyptic London** where Arthurian myth has bled into the modern world, and a combat system that lets Gwendolyn command the **spectral Knights of the Round Table** while fighting alongside them.

## 3. Release date: still TBA

As of **October 21, 2026**, there is **no official release date**. At Gamescom 2026, director **Ary Chen** declined to give a date, a playtime figure or a boss count, saying development was still ongoing and the team was gathering feedback.

- At the **February 2025 announcement**, the official window was **2026**.
- Some storefronts list **October 29, 2026** — this is a **placeholder date**, not an official release date. It happens to coincide with *Phantom Blade Zero*.
- Media speculation now mostly points to **2027**; IGN and Push Square both say "not before 2027."
- Wishlisting is open on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Steam and Epic Games Store.
- **Xbox Play Anywhere** is confirmed for the Xbox version.
- There is **no public demo download**. The 45-minute demo is available at events only, including **Beijing Fusion on October 24–25, 2026** (Booth A06).

Treat any specific date you see online as unconfirmed unless it comes from Eclipse Glow Games.

## 4. Story: London, Avalon and the Fog

The invasion comes from **the Otherworld**. London is broken apart, its streets fused with medieval ruins and a supernatural **Fog**. Beyond the city lies **Avalon**, the otherworldly realm that Gwendolyn must reach.

- **Gwendolyn** is the lone human survivor at the centre of the story. She inherits **King Arthur's power to command the Fog**.
- Her goal is the **Holy Grail**, which she believes can save her sister and restore the world.
- **Guinevere** is Arthur's queen: a silver-haired, blindfolded figure who emerges from the Fog and gives Gwendolyn the sword that commands the Round Table knights. She is **not** Gwendolyn's sister.
- The story deliberately mixes Arthurian legend with other mythologies. A demo boss fight against **Anubis** takes place in the **Great Russell Museum**, a location inspired by the British Museum; the game also features a **Guan Yu** encounter.
- **Guan Yu** appears as a mentor in the museum's **Chinese pavilion** — and speaks Chinese even in the English dub. The game's museum is built around multiple mythologies, including Egyptian and Japanese wings.
- The theory that Gwendolyn is Arthur's daughter is **unconfirmed**; neither the game nor the studio has stated it.

## 5. Characters and voice cast

- **Gwendolyn (格雯德琳)** — protagonist, voiced by **Angela Sant'Albano** (*Resident Evil Requiem*'s Grace Ashcroft). She replaced **Jennifer English** (*Clair Obscur: Expedition 33*), who stepped down for health reasons but remains involved as an advisor.
- **Guinevere (桂妮薇儿)** — the white-haired mentor figure introduced in the **"Beneath the Veil"** story trailer.
- **Niniane (宁妮安)** — a shapeshifting companion who can become a sword and helps with puzzles, exploration and secret routes.
- **The Knights of the Round Table** — spectral allies including **Lancelot**, **Gawain**, **Kay**, **Bedivere**, **Gaheris**, **Lamorak**, **Palamedes** and **Bors**. Eclipse Glow says there are **10+** in total, each with distinct abilities, elemental damage and combat roles.
- **Mordred**, **Anubis**, **Tyronoe** and **Geraint** — bosses shown or named in the Gamescom 2026 demo. Geraint is an optional boss in the museum's Japanese wing.

## 6. Combat: the Dual Frontline Battle System

The core system is the **Dual Frontline Battle System**. Gwendolyn fights on one line while commanding spectral knights on the other.

- **Two loadouts**, each with a **main and support slot** — four active knights in total — and slots can be swapped even mid-combo.
- **10+ Knights of the Round Table** to recruit, each with unique abilities, elemental damage and team roles (defensive, burst damage, flying). Defeating certain knights reportedly makes them **Sworn Knights**.
- Knights can be combined for **elemental reactions and extended combos**.
- **Niniane** can transform into a sword, adding a second weapon style and traversal/puzzle options.
- **Colossal Knights** the size of mountains serve as both bosses and explorable arenas.
- Combat is **fast and combo-driven**, with dodges, counters and weighty attacks — closer to *Devil May Cry* than *Dark Souls*.
- The game features **four weapon types**, **30+ bosses** and a **30+ hour campaign**, according to Eclipse Glow's own interviews. It is **not a Soulslike**; difficulty options and light RPG elements are included.

## 7. Gamescom 2026: the first public hands-on

At **Gamescom 2026** (Cologne, August 26–30, Hall 6, Booth B-050), Eclipse Glow showed the first European public demo and premiered the **"Beneath the Veil"** story trailer at Opening Night Live.

- The demo covered a story mission in ruined London and multi-phase boss fights, including **Mordred** (labelled "medium" difficulty), **Anubis** and **Tyronoe**.
- A 45-minute hands-on session was widely praised for its combat speed and knight-summoning system.
- The game won **"Most Epic"** at the Gamescom Awards 2026.
- PC demo stations ran on RTX 5080s with **DLSS 4.5 Dynamic Multi-Frame Generation**.
- The game supports **11 text languages**, including Brazilian Portuguese. Voice-over is **English and Simplified Chinese only**, so Japanese is text/UI only.

## 8. Why it matters for Chinese AAA games

Eclipse Glow Games is a **Chengdu-based studio of roughly 115–150 developers**, many with experience on *Assassin's Creed*, *Yakuza* and *Prince of Persia*. It is **majority-owned by Tencent** but self-publishes *Tides of Annihilation*.

The game is part of a wider wave of Chinese single-player AAA projects — alongside *Black Myth: Wukong*, *Phantom Blade Zero*, *WUCHANG: Fallen Feathers* and *Where Winds Meet* — that are moving away from mobile and live-service models toward premium, console-quality action games. Its Arthurian setting is also a departure: instead of Chinese mythology, it reworks a Western myth through a Chinese studio's action-design sensibilities.
