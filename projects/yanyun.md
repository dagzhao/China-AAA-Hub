# Where Winds Meet

> 📰 **Latest:** [Where Winds Meet 2.3: Qingzhou and DLSS 5](/news/where-winds-meet-2-3-qingzhou)

## 1. Historical Context: The Chaos of Yanyun
Set during the turbulent end of the Five Dynasties and Ten Kingdoms period, the game explores a world where old orders have crumbled. In the desolate lands of the Sixteen Prefectures of Yanyun, players take on the role of a wanderer forging their own path amidst the tides of history.

## 2. Core Philosophy: No Sects, Total Freedom
*Where Winds Meet* redefines the boundaries of open-world martial arts.
* **No Sect Restrictions**: Players are not bound to a single school. You can learn diverse techniques like Acupuncture and Tai Chi by "observing" others or challenging masters.
* **Environmental Interaction**: Elements like flowing water, falling leaves, and even animal behaviors can inspire your martial arts moves.
* **Oriental Wonders**: Beyond hard-hitting combat, the game integrates traditional cultural elements such as astrology and meridian systems.

## 4. Version 2.2: The Hidden Mountain Finale

**Version 2.2 "Perilous Eminence"** launched on **September 16, 2026** after a five-hour maintenance, closing the Hidden Mountain storyline:

- **Two campaigns** — **Skyward City Ruins**: ride wooden kites through collapsing towers and face the previous Grand Master, **Qing**. **Final Direction**: a battlefield where gravity keeps reversing, culminating in a duel with **Elder Peng**. **Qianye** returns as the finale's antagonist.
- **Two world bosses** — **Golden Colossus Taicu**, tied to Qin Shi Huang's twelve golden statues: destroy the field structures to lift its seal, then climb its body to break the core. **Goshawk**, a hammer-wielding boss whose molten-iron "flower" attacks light up the Hidden Mountain wilds.
- **Transdust Ruins (September 23)** — a four-phase extraction mode (prepare, search, contest, extract) built with an official **Escape from Tarkov** collaboration. Requires level 20+ and supports solo, three-player or AI teams; disguises, pickpocketing and acupoint strikes are all usable. The China server had already launched it on **July 10**.
- **Combat & season** — the **Drunken Fist** style arrived on **September 3**, alongside a **Yuen Woo-ping** choreography collaboration. The **Chilled Current** battle pass ran until **October 15**.
- **Reception** — most complaints centered on **Qianye's damage scaling** at high world levels and legendary difficulty, with some players calling her second phase "cheap." The game's overall Steam rating sat around **86% positive**; this is not a 2.2-specific score.

## 5. Version 2.3: Qingzhou, the First Thematic Region

**Version 2.3 "Friends From Afar"** arrived on **October 15, 2026** — the game's first "thematic region," smaller than a full major region but denser in systems:

- **Qingzhou** — the fifth major area, covering **Mount Tai**, **Wellspring Town**, **Crownleek Terrace** and the **Yellow River estuary**.
- **New school: Inkbound Order (文津馆)** — introduces a dual-school / visiting-disciple system and a card-debate PvP mode, **"A Duel of Wits."**
- **New weapon: Ribbon Drum (舞绫鼓)** — paired with the **Silkbind: Wings (牵丝·翊)** style, using long ribbons for crowd control and drum beats to buff allies.
- **Three world bosses** — **Tidewatcher (观潮客)**, **Old Raftsman (筏师的爷爷)** and **Shuttlecock Champion (祝英晗)**.
- **Progression & events** — solo level cap raised from 17 to 18; Season 5 **"Ever Aflame"**. Qingzhou's opening month and **Exam Showdown** run **October 15–November 14**; **New Armament Drill** runs **October 15–29**; **Bubble Party** and **Merchant's Way** open **October 22**; **Penglai Isle** and the **Mount Tai Blizzard** campaign arrive **October 29**.
- **DLSS 5** — announced on **October 13–14**, with full **DLSS 5 (3D-Guided Neural Rendering)** coming to the PC version by **late November**. *Where Winds Meet* is one of the first three games in the world to support it.
- **Reception** — some players feel Qingzhou is more of a "themed mini-map" than a full region; performance and shader-compilation stutters were also raised, and DLSS 5 was not available at launch.

## 6. China Server: Running About Half a Year Ahead

The China server has its own, faster content cadence:

- **Version 2.8.5 "平澜起风波" (September 24, 2026)** — completes Hangzhou with the **Pinglan Academy** and **Yingtian Pagoda**, plus the mulberry-sea and Qiantang embankment areas. The main story and five side stories exceed **500 minutes**. New guardian **Qiantang Tide**, world boss **Angry Tiger**, and new trial content. The client also shed nearly **100 GB on PC** and **20 GB on mobile**.
- **"长乐夜未央" (October 23, 2026)** — the second thematic region, reached by "golden ship" from Hangzhou. It adds **four subregions, two guardians, three trails and five world bosses**, including the game's **first freely explorable underwater world** with water-and-land layered maps, a tide cycle, "everything is tradeable" markets, and a black market. The theme song **"宝船"** is by **DOUDOU**.
- **Why it matters** — China-only content tends to reach the international build roughly **six months later**, so these systems are a preview of what global players can expect in 2027.

## 7. Audience, Awards and Crossovers

- **100 million players** — On **August 26, 2026**, NetEase announced **100 million cumulative registered players**, up from 80 million+ in June. This is registrations, not monthly active users.
- **Mobile revenue** — About **$223.7 million** by **June 16, 2026** (AppMagic estimate), including roughly **$94.2 million** since the December 2025 global mobile launch; China accounts for about **77%** of recent mobile revenue.
- **Steam** — The all-time peak was around **250,600** at the November 2025 launch; the **October 2026 peak is 20,678**, with a monthly average of about **8,647**. Ratings sit at **86–87% positive** across 113,000–116,000 reviews; Metacritic is around **71** from critics and **6.9–7.2** from users.
- **Awards** — At Gamescom 2026 the game won **Best Mobile Game** and used the show to announce the 100-million milestone. It did not appear at TGS 2026.
- **Crossovers** — *The Legend of White Snake* event "牵缘记" launched **August 2**; KFC ran a 400-store campaign in March; McDonald's ran a Mid-Autumn collaboration from **September 16 to October 7**; Taiwan's icash 2.0 released eight themed cards from **September 30 to December 31**.
- **Switch 2** — No announcement. Producer **Joy Chen** has said the team has heard the requests but will only expand to new hardware when it can deliver a **fully equivalent experience**, not merely to hit a deadline.
