## 1. Lore: Ancient Shu & Featherization
The story takes place during the turbulent final years of the Ming Dynasty in the land of Bashu. Beyond the historical chaos lies the "Featherization" plague—a grotesque mutation linked to the mysterious Sanxingdui civilization that transforms people into eldritch horrors instead of immortals.

## 2. Core Elements: Qi & Eldritch Powers
* **Sanxingdui Aesthetics**: Enemy and environment designs are deeply integrated with elements from Shu civilization, such as bronze trees and golden masks.
* **Brutal Combat**: Players take on the role of Wuchang, a memory-lost warrior navigating a blend of cold weapons and early firearms.
* **Cthulhu-inspired Fantasy**: Beneath the traditional wuxia exterior lies an unspeakable terror of ancient gods and physical mutations.

## 3. Now Available: 5 Million Players & a Sequel in Development

**WUCHANG: Fallen Feathers** launched on **July 24, 2025** for **PC, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X|S**, and was included in **Xbox Game Pass on day one**.

One year after launch, the game has surpassed **5 million players worldwide**. To celebrate the first anniversary, all editions received a **30% discount — the lowest price ever** (standard edition from **¥248** to **¥173.6**).

**Post-Launch Content:**
* **Deluxe Expansion** (February 2026): additional expansion content for owners of the deluxe edition.
* **"Night & White" DLC** (July 23, 2026): themed outfits and weapon packs inspired by the Black and White Impermanence (Heibai Wuchang).

**Sequel Officially Announced (July 29, 2026):** Publisher **505 Games** and its parent **Digital Bros** have signed an agreement for a **new chapter of the WUCHANG series**. The sequel is led by original creator and director **Xia Siyuan** through his new studio **Indolphinity**, with 505 Games providing up to **€21.5 million** in funding and handling global publishing. After the original Leenzee development team dissolved, Digital Bros **acquired the IP in April 2026** and has set up a dedicated subsidiary in **Chengdu** to work closely with Indolphinity. No title or release window has been announced yet.

> Xia Siyuan: "*Wuchang's story is far from over.*"

## 4. The Sequel: Bai Wuchang Returns

The new chapter is moving from announcement to production — slowly, and with the original director at the helm.

- **Studio & IP** — Xia Siyuan's new studio, **Indolphinity** (Chengdu Digui Haitun Technology), was registered on **January 22, 2026**, with Xia holding **99%** and serving as legal representative. On **April 27–28**, Digital Bros bought the complete *WUCHANG* IP from Leenzee for about **€4 million**, clearing future royalty payments before setting up the sequel.
- **Deal terms** — The **July 29, 2026** agreement provides up to **€21.5 million** in first-phase funding, with 505 Games handling global publishing; development remains in China. Both sides describe the project as being in a **preparation phase**.
- **What we know** — At ChinaJoy in early August, Xia confirmed **Bai Wuchang returns as protagonist**, the sequel is at an early stage with core systems being built, and **some former Leenzee staff have rejoined** — though "the whole team returning" is not accurate. A DLC for the first game has **not been ruled out**, but the sequel comes first.
- **Tone** — Asked about the original's online controversies, Xia said he has **"no regrets, only gratitude."** In a September 14 comment marking 505's 20th anniversary, he wrote: **"I get it, friend — no rush, but it has to be fast."**
- **Hiring & finances** — Indolphinity opened **more than ten roles** in August (3D scanning, audio lead, technical art, level design, animation lead, environment art, IT, finance) at **¥5K–40K/month**. On **September 24**, Digital Bros reported FY2026 revenue of **€108.5 million (+16%)**, EBITDA of **€54.2 million (+62%)** and a narrowed net loss of **€6.5 million**, naming *WUCHANG* as a main driver.
- **No date** — No title, platforms or release window. The Chengdu subsidiary has been announced, but its formal registration and scale remain unconfirmed.

## 5. Post-Launch: No New Patches, Still on Game Pass

Despite the sequel news, the original game has been quiet:

- **Last update** — The final official patch was **1.7.1 on November 20–21, 2025**, adding three armor sets, two weapons, four free headwear items, boss rematches and performance fixes. There have been **no official patches, balance changes, outfits or DLC from August to October 2026**.
- **The rumored 1.8** — An NGA leak claims a 1.8 update with difficulty options, Boss Rush and photo mode is "basically finished, only testing left," but 505 and Leenzee have not confirmed it; the leaker says it may never ship or be folded into a "2.0."
- **Game Pass** — Still in the library: it joined Ultimate/PC on day one (July 24, 2025), was added to Premium on **May 6, 2026**, and does not appear on the October 15 or October 31, 2026 departure lists.
- **PS Plus** — The PS5 version was a **PlayStation Plus Essential** monthly game from **May 5 to June 1, 2026**.

## 6. By the Numbers: 1 Million Sold, 5 Million Players, a Mixed Reception

- **Sales** — Official figures: **1 million copies sold by March 31, 2026**, with more than **€30 million** in net revenue in eight months. The "5 million" milestone refers to **players**, not sales, and includes Game Pass.
- **Steam** — Launch peak **131,000** concurrent players. The overall user rating remains **Mixed (about 52–57% positive)**, in sharp contrast with the **~80% recent** score; at launch the positive rate briefly fell to **13–19%**.
- **Sequel bump** — After the sequel announcement, concurrent players climbed back to **5,004**, a 2026 high.
- **Discounts** — A 30% anniversary sale ran **July 24–August 7**; 505's 20th-anniversary sale repeated 30% off from **September 10–24**.
- **Platforms** — The PS5 version carried the **PS5 Pro Enhanced** label from launch, with three modes and roughly 15–20 more frames on Pro. There is **no Switch 2 announcement**.
- **What happened to Leenzee** — April reports said Leenzee asked the team to convert to outsourcing; after the refusal the team was dissolved around the Spring Festival, and Xia Siyuan departed. Some staff began labor arbitration over unpaid wages or compensation. Leenzee itself remains registered in Chengdu, so claims that it "went bankrupt" are unsupported. The planned post-launch DLC, new weapons and boss-rush content were cancelled with the dissolution; **1.7.1 is effectively the final official content**.
- **No shows** — No Gamescom 2026 or TGS 2026 presence was found; the main August appearance was ChinaJoy, where Xia gave sequel interviews.
