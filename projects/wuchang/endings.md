# WUCHANG: Fallen Feathers — All Endings & True Ending Guide

*WUCHANG: Fallen Feathers* has **four endings**. Two of them are easy to miss, and the community does not fully agree on which one deserves the label **"true ending."** This guide gives the exact conditions, the point of no return, and a save-backup plan so you can see every ending in one playthrough.

## Quick answer

| Ending | Type | Key requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Successor of the Bo** | Secret / early | Complete the Bo Magus questline, then refuse the Storyteller at 丹青苑 (Annalum) |
| **Jarful of Medicine** | Bad / default | Give the **Jade Feather Chisel** to Xuanyangzi and choose the Cycle of Samsara |
| **Dream's Sweet Deceit** | Hidden dream ending (called "true" by some guides) | Collect **all five chisels**, complete both rings, forge the **Ring of Samsara – Rebirth**, choose the Realm of Dreams |
| **Bound by Fate's Threads** | Good / narrative true ending | Keep the chisel, complete Fang Yao and He Youzai's quests and keep them alive, then choose the Realm of Truth **without** the Rebirth ring |

## The five chisels

To unlock **Dream's Sweet Deceit**, you need **all five chisels**. Do **not** give the Jade Feather Chisel to Xuanyangzi, or you will be one short.

| Chisel | Source | Effect |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Starting chisel | Owned from the start | Returns you to the last dream point and strengthens you |
| **Jade Feather Chisel** | Chapter 2 boss **Perfect Bride Fang Ling** | Unlocks "Words of the Former Gods" at shrines |
| **Golden Feather Chisel** | Chapter 3 boss **Zhang Xianzhong** | Maxes the Feather Dew Flask's healing |
| **Crimson Feather Chisel** | Chapter 4 boss **Chongzhen Emperor Zhu Youjian** | Raises weapon proficiency to +10 |
| **Azure Feather Chisel** | Chapter 5 hidden boss **Zhao Yun** (Mt. Zhenwu, Avian Dwelling, Cenotaph) | Unlocks the final feather-quenching slot |

> The Azure Feather Chisel requires progressing Zhao Yun's dialogue, then returning to the Avian Dwelling cenotaph after defeating Zhu Youjian.

## 1. Successor of the Bo (secret ending)

This is the earliest ending and the easiest to lock yourself out of.

1. In **Chapter 3**, meet **Bo Magus / Red-Clad Lady** at **Avian Dwelling** and **agree** to her proposal.
2. In **Chapter 4**, reach **丹青苑 (Annalum)** after the **Fierce Tiger** boss in **Mt. Zhenwu**.
3. When the **Storyteller** asks whether to continue, **refuse**. Do not select "Continue Forward."
4. Defeat **Bo Magus (the Bo shaman)**.

The game ends early and cuts off the Bo Capital. This ending has its own trophy, **Successor of the Bo**.

> **Point of no return:** the Storyteller conversation at 丹青苑. Finish all Mt. Zhenwu and Worship's Rise exploration and NPC quests before saying yes.

## 2. Jarful of Medicine (bad / default ending)

This is the default outcome if you do not complete the ring quests.

1. In **Chapter 4**, on the road from **Puyuan Temple** to the **Burning Cave**, meet **Xuanyangzi**.
2. Give him the **Jade Feather Chisel** when asked.
3. Do not complete the **He Youzai** and **Fang Yao** questlines, so you cannot forge the Rebirth ring.
4. Defeat the final boss.
5. At the Gate of Truth, choose **"Enter the Cycle of Samsara."**

This is the "bad" ending in most guides. The trophy is **Jarful of Medicine**.

## 3. Dream's Sweet Deceit (hidden dream ending)

This is the most demanding ending and the one Japanese guides often call the **true ending** (Japanese trophy: まるで夢のよう).

1. **Collect all five chisels** (see the table above).
2. **Keep the Jade Feather Chisel.** At the Xuanyangzi conversation, choose **"Remain Silent"** instead of handing it over.
3. Complete **He Youzai's** and **Fang Yao's** questlines and **keep both alive**.
   - Fang Yao's quest requires the **Shu Silk Scarf** from **Hillswatch** and a fight at her door in **Cloudspire**.
4. Obtain both **Ring of Samsara – Cause** and **Ring of Samsara – Effect** (Chapter 4, Puyuan Temple).
5. At the third mystery gate in **Capital Depths** (Bo Capital), intentionally choose the "wrong" answer **"Re-enter the Cycle" (再入輪迴)** rather than "The Great Dream Awakens."
6. Defeat **Demon of Obsession** at the **Shu God's Dwelling**.
7. At the altar in the **Shu God's Dwelling**, combine Cause + Effect into the **Ring of Samsara – Rebirth**.
8. Defeat the final boss, **Xuanyangzi**, while the Rebirth ring is in your inventory.
9. At the gate, choose **"Enter the Realm of Dreams."** The sisters board a ship together.

The trophy is **Dream's Sweet Deceit**.

## 4. Bound by Fate's Threads (good / narrative true ending)

GameSpot and some other guides call this the **true ending** (「命裏無時」).

1. Follow the same steps as **Dream's Sweet Deceit** up to the final boss. **Four or five chisels are both fine**; the Azure Feather Chisel is not required.
2. Keep **Fang Yao and He Youzai alive**.
3. Before interacting with the gate, make sure the **Ring of Samsara – Rebirth is NOT in your inventory**. Consume it from the item menu, discard it, or never forge it.
4. Defeat **Xuanyangzi**.
5. At the gate, choose **"Enter the Realm of Truth" (進入真實之境)**. The feathers on your left arm disappear, symbolising freedom from the Bo.

The trophy is **Bound by Fate's Threads**.

## Which ending is the "true" ending?

**The game does not officially define a "true ending."** All four endings have their own trophies. There are two competing answers:

- **GameSpot and some guides:** **Bound by Fate's Threads** is the true ending — it enters the Realm of Truth and removes the feathers, which reads as the narrative truth.
- **TheGamer, DualShockers, PowerPyx, Japanese and some Chinese guides:** **Dream's Sweet Deceit** is the true ending — it has the hardest unlock conditions (five chisels + both rings + Rebirth ring + Demon of Obsession).

The narrative reading and the difficulty reading produce different answers. The English name *Dream's Sweet Deceit* also hints that it is still a dream. Treat "true ending" as a community label.

## See all endings in one playthrough

Manual save backup is the standard method:

1. **Before the Storyteller decision at 丹青苑** — for the secret ending.
2. **Before the Xuanyangzi conversation at the Burning Cave** — for the Jade Feather Chisel choice.
3. **After the final boss, before touching the gate** — for the Dream/Truth split.

On PC, the save folder is under:

`C:\Users\[username]\AppData\Local\Project_Plague\Saved`

Back up the folder before each branch, then restore it to replay the final choice. On console, use the platform's cloud/manual save tools.

## FAQ

**Can I get all four endings in one playthrough?**
Yes, with manual save backups. Without backups, you need at least two playthroughs because the Jade Feather Chisel and Rebirth ring decisions are irreversible.

**Does giving the Jade Feather Chisel to Xuanyangzi lock me out of the good ending?**
Yes. Giving it to him leads to the **Jarful of Medicine** ending. Choose "Remain Silent" to keep it.

**Is Dream's Sweet Deceit or Bound by Fate's Threads the true ending?**
It depends on the guide. GameSpot and some guides call **Bound by Fate's Threads** the true ending; TheGamer, DualShockers, PowerPyx and Japanese guides often call **Dream's Sweet Deceit** the true ending. Both are valid, and both are tracked by trophies.

**Do I need the DLC to see the endings?**
No. All four endings are in the base game.
