# Culture 101

Chinese AAA games don't just borrow from myth — they run on it. To read the **Logic of the East**, work through the four modules below.

Every module is built the same way: **what you see on screen**, **how the pieces connect**, and **where the ideas came from**. Depth is folded away behind panels, so you can skim a module in two minutes or read it for twenty.

### 🎓 Modules

| Module | Topic | Why it matters |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **01** | **[The Taoism Code](./taoism)** | Yin-yang, the five phases, qi, and talismans — why combat in these games is metaphysics. |
| **02** | **[The Three Realms](./three-realms)** | Heaven, earth, and the underworld as three jurisdictions — and why the cosmos is an org chart. |
| **03** | **[Samsara & Karma](./karma)** | Death as a checkpoint, and a ledger that balances itself. Why nobody gets to win and be finished. |
| **04** | **[Beasts & Myths](./beasts)** | The shared bestiary behind every boss, and why Chinese monsters are researched rather than invented. |
